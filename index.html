01 / FORMAT

A specific export path

The source-backed Gumroad path expects the exact ordered 72-column purchase export and final Totals row from a pinned exporter revision. It covers positive, unrefunded, undisputed USD purchases with zero tax, shipping, tips and extra affiliate or processor charges.

Custom or reordered columns, refunds, disputes, discounts, subscriptions, variants, buyer-currency or presentment fields, and other unsupported scenarios are refused on this path.

Legacy Etsy/Gumroad schemas also exist, but Etsy’s exact live format and current real-seller export compatibility remain unverified. A marketplace name alone does not establish support.

02 / MEANING

Export income is not profit

Net After Fees is an export subtotal, not total business profit or taxable income. Business expenses outside the export are not included.

For periods with missing or partial fee data, Net After Fees and reserve illustrations are withheld. Available partial fees are marked incomplete.

Recognized fields and supported totals are checked, but those checks do not certify that a source export is complete or correct. There is no bank sync, inventory tracking or currency conversion.

03 / ASSUMPTIONS

Illustrations, not a tax answer.

The sample’s 10%, 19% and 28% scenario rates are arbitrary assumptions in simplified SE-based arithmetic. They are not verified tax brackets, and no scenario is recommended.

The figures are not tax estimates, amounts owed, required payments, recommended savings or an upper bound on tax. A zero illustration does not establish zero tax liability.

Excluded: annual thresholds and limits, wage coordination, Additional Medicare Tax, filing status, other income, deductions, credits and withholding. This is not tax advice, a complete Schedule C or a substitute for a qualified preparer.
